01 Solution

With us, technology and instruction are part of the same system.

A complete training system from a single supplier, for its entire life cycle. No gaps between those who build, those who maintain and those who instruct, and no surprises on the day the pilot steps into the cockpit.

  1. 01

    Planning

    Architectural and functional planning of the training center to the instructional requirement.

  2. 02

    Construction

    Building the training environment and integrating computing and communications systems in compliance with regulatory requirements.

  3. 03

    Simulator development

    A simulator tailored to the aircraft and to the customer's unique configuration.

  4. 04

    Operation and maintenance

    Configuration management, maintenance and operational support throughout the system's life.

  5. 05
